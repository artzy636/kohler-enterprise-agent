# Remote Work Policy

Employees in eligible roles may work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to manager approval and role requirements (customer-facing and lab-based roles are generally excluded). Fully remote arrangements require VP-level approval and are reviewed annually. Remote employees must maintain core collaboration hours of 10am-3pm in their local time zone and ensure a secure home network per IT security guidelines. Equipment for remote setups (monitor, chair, keyboard) can be reimbursed up to $300 per employee every 3 years through the standard expense process.
