# Parental Leave Policy

Kohler offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave for the primary caregiver and 6 weeks for the secondary caregiver, applicable to birth, adoption, or foster placement. Employees must have completed at least 6 months of continuous service to be eligible. Leave can be taken continuously or split into two blocks within the first 12 months after the qualifying event, with HR approval. Health benefits continue uninterrupted during parental leave. Employees intending to take parental leave should notify their manager and HR at least 30 days in advance where possible.
