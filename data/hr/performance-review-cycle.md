# Performance Review Cycle

Performance reviews are conducted twice yearly, in June and December. Employees are rated on a 5-point scale: 1 (Needs Improvement) to 5 (Outstanding), based on goal achievement and core competencies. Self-assessments are due two weeks before the review meeting, followed by manager assessment and a calibration session across the team. Ratings feed into compensation decisions and promotion eligibility. Employees who receive a rating of 2 or below are placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) with a 60-day review checkpoint.
