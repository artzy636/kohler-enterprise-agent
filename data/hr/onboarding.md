# New Hire Onboarding

New employees complete a 2-week onboarding program covering company systems access, benefits enrollment, compliance training (data privacy, code of conduct, anti-harassment), and role-specific training with their manager. IT equipment is provisioned before day one. A dedicated onboarding buddy is assigned for the first 30 days. Formal 30-60-90 day check-ins are scheduled with the hiring manager to track integration progress. Benefits enrollment must be completed within the first 30 days of employment or the employee must wait until the next open enrollment period.
