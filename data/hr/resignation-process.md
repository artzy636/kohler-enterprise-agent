# Resignation and Offboarding Process

Employees resigning are asked to provide a minimum of 2 weeks' notice (4 weeks for management-level roles) to ensure a smooth transition. Upon resignation, HR schedules an exit interview and coordinates the return of company equipment. Final paycheck, including any accrued and unused leave payout, is processed within the next standard payroll cycle following the last working day. Access to company systems is revoked at end of business on the employee's last day. Rehire eligibility is evaluated case by case and noted in the employee's offboarding record.
