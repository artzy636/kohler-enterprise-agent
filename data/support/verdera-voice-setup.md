# Verdera Voice Mirror — Setup Guide

The Verdera Voice mirror connects to home Wi-Fi through the Konnect app for voice-activated lighting control. During setup, mount the mirror per the included instructions, power it on, and add it as a new device in the Konnect app. Voice activation requires linking to a compatible smart home voice assistant through the app's integration settings. Once connected, users can adjust lighting brightness and color temperature hands-free, useful for tasks like shaving or applying makeup where reaching for a switch is inconvenient.
