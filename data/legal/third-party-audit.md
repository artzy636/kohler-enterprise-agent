# Third-Party Compliance Audit Process

High-risk vendors and manufacturing partners are subject to an annual third-party compliance audit covering labor standards, environmental practices, and data security controls, conducted by an independent auditing firm. Audit findings are shared with the vendor with a required corrective action plan for any major findings, due within 60 days. Repeated failure to remediate major findings across two consecutive audit cycles results in vendor relationship review by the executive compliance committee, which may lead to contract termination.
