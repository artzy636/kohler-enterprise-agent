# Contract Review and Approval Workflow

All external contracts must be reviewed by Legal before signature, regardless of value. Standard-template contracts with no modifications can be approved by Legal within 5 business days. Contracts with non-standard terms, liability caps above $1M, or exclusivity clauses require additional Legal director review and can take 2-3 weeks. No employee other than designated signing authorities (VP level and above, or delegated by Legal) may sign a binding contract on behalf of Kohler.
