# Data Privacy Policy

Kohler collects and processes personal data (employee, customer, and partner data) only for legitimate business purposes and in compliance with applicable regional privacy regulations. Personal data must be stored only in approved, encrypted systems; storing customer or employee data on personal devices or unapproved cloud services is prohibited. Data subject access requests must be routed to the Legal/Compliance team and fulfilled within 30 days. Any suspected data privacy violation should be reported immediately per the breach reporting policy.
