# Intellectual Property Policy

All work product, inventions, designs, and code created by employees within the scope of their employment are the property of Kohler, per the IP assignment agreement signed at hiring. Employees must disclose any pre-existing IP or side projects that may overlap with their role before starting. Use of open-source software in Kohler products must be reviewed and approved by Legal to ensure license compatibility. Employees are prohibited from using Kohler confidential designs or code in any external or personal project, including after employment ends.
