# Regulatory Compliance Overview

Kohler's products and operations are subject to regulatory frameworks including consumer product safety standards, environmental regulations (water efficiency certifications for plumbing products), and data privacy law across the regions where it operates. The Compliance team maintains a regulatory tracking calendar and coordinates cross-functional response to new or changing regulations. Product teams must complete a compliance review checklist before launching any new connected or water-using product, coordinated with both Legal and Engineering.
