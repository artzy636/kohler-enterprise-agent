# Data Retention Policy

Customer transaction and support data is retained for 7 years to meet regulatory and warranty obligations, after which it is securely deleted. Employee records, including background check data, are retained for the duration of employment plus 3 years for legal purposes. Marketing and non-essential customer engagement data is retained for 2 years unless the customer has an active account. Data subject to a legal hold is retained indefinitely until Legal releases the hold, overriding standard retention schedules.
