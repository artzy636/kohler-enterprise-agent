# Year-End Financial Closing Process

Finance closes the fiscal year on December 31, with a blackout period from December 15 to January 2 during which non-critical purchase orders and expense reimbursements are paused. All outstanding expense reports for the current fiscal year must be submitted by December 10 to be included in that year's closing. Departments should finalize any budget rollover requests by December 5. Final year-end financial statements are published internally by January 20 following audit review.
