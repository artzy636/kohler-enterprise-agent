# Petty Cash Policy

Each office location maintains a petty cash fund of up to $500 for small, immediate business expenses under $50 (office supplies, small client hospitality items). Petty cash disbursements require a receipt and a completed petty cash voucher signed by the office manager. The fund is reconciled monthly by Finance, and any discrepancies over $10 must be investigated and documented. Petty cash cannot be used for travel expenses, employee reimbursements exceeding $50, or recurring vendor payments — those follow the standard expense or invoice process.
