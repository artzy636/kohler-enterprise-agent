# Corporate Card Policy

Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel frequently or manage recurring vendor relationships, subject to manager and Finance approval. Cardholders must submit itemized receipts and expense categorization within 15 days of each statement close. Personal use of the corporate card is prohibited and may result in card revocation. Cards have a default monthly limit of $5,000, adjustable by Finance based on role and travel frequency. Lost or stolen cards must be reported to the card issuer and Finance immediately to avoid liability for unauthorized charges.
