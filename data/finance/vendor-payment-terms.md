# Standard Vendor Payment Terms

Standard payment terms for new vendors are net-30 from invoice receipt, contingent on a signed master service agreement and completed vendor onboarding in the procurement system. Preferred vendors with a 2+ year relationship and consistent on-time delivery may be extended net-45 terms upon Finance director approval. Early payment discounts offered by vendors should be evaluated by the requesting department and Finance before acceptance. International vendor payments are processed via wire transfer and may take an additional 3-5 business days to clear.
