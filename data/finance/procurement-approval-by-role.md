# Procurement and Purchase Approval Thresholds

Purchase approval authority is tiered by role: Individual contributors can approve purchases up to $500 without additional sign-off. Team leads can approve up to $2,000. Managers can approve up to $10,000. Directors and above can approve unlimited purchases within their department's approved annual budget. Any purchase exceeding $10,000, regardless of role, requires a second signature from Finance and a competitive quote comparison. Purchases must reference an active budget line; unbudgeted purchases require a budget exception request.
