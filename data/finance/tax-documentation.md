# Employee Tax Documentation Requirements

Employees must submit updated tax withholding forms within 30 days of any change in filing status or dependents. Annual tax documents are issued by January 31 each year through the payroll portal. Employees working across state or country lines for more than 30 days in a calendar year should notify Finance to ensure correct tax jurisdiction withholding. Independent contractors and vendors must submit a completed tax form before their first invoice payment is processed.
