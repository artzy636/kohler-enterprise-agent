# Employee Relocation Reimbursement

Employees relocating for a company-initiated transfer are eligible for reimbursement of reasonable moving expenses, including packing/shipping of household goods, temporary housing for up to 30 days, and one house-hunting trip for the employee and spouse/partner. Relocation reimbursement is capped at $15,000 domestically and $25,000 internationally, subject to VP approval. Employees who voluntarily leave the company within 12 months of a company-funded relocation are required to repay a prorated portion of the relocation benefit.
