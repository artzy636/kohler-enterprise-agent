# Capital Asset Purchase Approval

Purchases of capital equipment or assets valued over $10,000 with a useful life beyond one year require a capital expenditure (CapEx) request, reviewed by Finance and approved by the relevant VP. CapEx requests must include a business case, expected ROI or operational justification, and depreciation schedule. Asset purchases between $10,000 and $50,000 require VP approval; purchases above $50,000 require executive committee approval. All capital assets are tagged and tracked in the fixed asset register upon receipt.
